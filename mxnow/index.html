MXNOW Support

Questions about MXNOW? Write to support@onsidesoftware.com.

MXNOW gets you speaking Mexican Spanish out loud, with an AI conversation partner and the recorded voices of three native speakers.

Common questions

Which phones can hold conversations?

On iPhone: iOS 26 or later. On Android: Android 13 or later. Conversations need an internet connection; without one, Oído mexicano, the word lists and the drills still work.

My partner can't hear me.

Allow MXNOW to use the microphone and speech recognition. On iPhone: Settings > Apps > MXNOW. On Android: Settings > Apps > MXNOW > Permissions. An Android phone also needs its offline Spanish language, which MXNOW's settings can download. You can always type instead.

What is the 30-Day Travel Pass?

A single payment for 30 days of everything a subscription opens, made for a trip to Mexico. It doesn’t renew, so there is nothing to cancel. Its 30 days start when you buy it, and a second pass bought before the first ends adds 30 more. On Android, Google Play sells the pass as one calendar month instead of 30 days. The pass comes with Plan de viaje, in MXNOW’s ☰ menu: thirty days of short steps that follow a trip, from landing to flying home. Each day is built around 15 minutes, and you get the most out of it if you can spend closer to 30.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

My subscription or Travel Pass is missing on a new phone.

Open MXNOW's settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your progress is saved only on your phone, so it moves with your phone's own backup and restore (iCloud or Google). Deleting the app deletes it.

Whose voices are these?

Oído mexicano plays real recordings of three native speakers, from Mexico City, Culiacán and Ciudad Juárez. Words and phrases are read by a recorded computer voice. Your partner's replies are read aloud by Mexican voices from Microsoft's Azure speech service.

The partner said something wrong or offensive.

Tap Report this reply under the reply. The partner is generated by AI and can make mistakes, so treat its notes as practice, not as a final answer.

Is my conversation private?

What you say in a conversation goes through OnSide's service to Anthropic and Microsoft to get each reply and read it aloud, and OnSide keeps none of it. The Privacy Policy explains the details.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle refunds for purchases made through their stores. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, request one from your Google Play order history.
