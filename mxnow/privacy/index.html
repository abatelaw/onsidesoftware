MXNOW Privacy Policy

What stays on your phone, what goes where, and the little information we receive.

Effective date: November 27, 2026

MXNOW keeps your progress on your phone. To give you a conversation partner, what you say in a conversation goes through OnSide's service to Anthropic and Microsoft, and OnSide keeps none of it. This policy explains what stays on your phone, what goes where, and the little information we do receive.

1. Who we are

OnSide Software, Inc. ("OnSide," "we") makes MXNOW, an app for practicing spoken Mexican Spanish on iPhone and Android. This policy covers the MXNOW app and its pages at onsidesoftware.com/mxnow/. The rest of our website and our other products are covered by the OnSide Privacy Notice.

2. What stays on your phone

MXNOW saves these only in the app on your phone:

  • your progress: the lessons you finish, your Échale ganas list, your listening progress and your settings;
  • the words, phrases and modismos you add.

MXNOW has no account, and none of this is sent to OnSide, so we cannot see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it. Your phone may copy it into its own backups, such as iCloud or Google backup, under the backup settings you choose.

3. Your voice and the microphone

MXNOW uses the microphone only while you speak to it, to turn your Spanish into text. The recording is not kept.

  • On iPhone, Apple's speech recognition does this on the phone when the phone supports Spanish (Mexico). Otherwise Apple processes the audio on its servers, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • On Android, the phone's speech recognizer does this on the phone with its offline Spanish language. On a phone without it, you can download the language in MXNOW's settings where the phone offers it, or type instead; your voice is not sent off the phone.

You can turn off microphone access in your phone's settings at any time and type instead.

4. Your conversation partner

Your AI conversation partner, and the coach that corrects your Spanish, are Claude, made by Anthropic. When you talk with your partner, MXNOW sends your lines, your partner's earlier lines and the lesson's setup (including any words and phrases you added to that lesson) through OnSide's partner service to Anthropic, to get each reply and each correction. Your partner's replies, and other Spanish that does not come recorded in the app, are read aloud by Microsoft's Azure speech service: MXNOW sends the text to be read through the same service and saves the audio on your phone. OnSide's service passes these requests on and keeps none of them; Cloudflare, which runs it for us, keeps a log of requests (their time and result, not what was said) for up to 7 days. Each request carries a random number MXNOW makes when it is installed, used only to limit how many requests a phone can send each minute; it is not your name or your phone's identifier. Anthropic and Microsoft process these requests for us under their terms for business customers, which do not allow them to use your conversations to train their AI models.

5. Purchases

Apple (the App Store) or Google (Google Play) handles your subscription and your payment. We never receive your card or bank details. The store gives us the purchase records it gives every app seller, such as the plan, the date, the price and the country. MXNOW checks your subscription with the store from your phone. Apple's and Google's privacy policies cover what they collect.

6. Crash reports and statistics

If you agree, in your phone's settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple or Google may give us crash reports and usage statistics about MXNOW. They do not include your name or contact details. MXNOW contains no advertising or tracking code. If you leave Share anonymous counts on in MXNOW's settings, MXNOW sends us, once a day, counts such as how many of your partner's replies needed a fix and how often each coaching check was used: names and numbers only, with no words you or your partner said, no device model and no identifier. We add them to that day's totals for all phones on the same app version and keep the totals for 2 years. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

When MXNOW opens, it asks OnSide’s service whether a newer version of MXNOW is available and for any updated instructions for your conversation partner. That request says only whether your phone is an iPhone or an Android phone and which version of MXNOW it runs: no identifier, nothing about you and nothing you said.

7. Replies and corrections you report

If you tap Report this reply, MXNOW sends us the reply, the lesson it came from, the reason you pick, and the app version and phone model, so that we can correct the partner. It sends no name, email address or other identifier. We keep reports for 2 years. Our service provider, Cloudflare, receives reports for us; we do not store the internet address a report came from, but Cloudflare's logs, which we use to fix problems with the service, may keep it for up to 7 days.

If you tap This correction is wrong?, MXNOW sends us what you said, the correction it showed and its explanation, which check made it, the lesson, and the app version and phone model, so that we can fix the coach. It sends no name or other identifier. We keep these reports for 2 years and handle them the same way as reported replies.

8. When you write to us

If you email support@onsidesoftware.com, we receive your email address and what you send. We use it to answer you and to fix problems, and we keep it for 2 years after your request is closed.

9. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use your conversations to train AI. MXNOW shows no ads.

10. Children

MXNOW is made for adults 18 and over. It is not directed to children under 13. MXNOW asks for no account, name or contact details. If you believe a child has written to us, tell us at privacy@onsidesoftware.com and we will delete the message.

11. Your choices and rights

What MXNOW keeps on your phone is yours to control: deleting the app deletes it. For information we hold about you, such as a support email, you may ask us to tell you what it is, to correct it or to delete it, by writing to privacy@onsidesoftware.com. We will respond within 45 days. Depending on where you live, the law may give you other rights, and we will honor them as that law requires.

12. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

13. Where we are

OnSide is in the United States, and the messages you send us are handled there.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change is material, we will also say so in the app.
