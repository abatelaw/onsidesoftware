OnSide Software Announces OnTime, OnTrac and OnFile: Software That Lets Lawyers Work Smarter, Not Harder

Three patent-pending applications for Mac and Windows, and the OnTrial Notebook, the ultimate digital trial notebook, expected December 1, 2026.

PHOENIX, Ariz., October 19, 2026 — OnSide Software, Inc., a software company founded by an attorney with more than thirty years of experience in the practice of law, today announced three applications for lawyers and legal teams: OnTime, OnTrac and OnFile. All three are currently expected to be available for Mac and Windows on December 1, 2026, sold directly at onsidesoftware.com. The OnTrial Notebook, the company’s ultimate digital trial notebook, is expected to be available the same day.

Each application runs quietly in the background and takes a routine chore off a lawyer’s desk:

  • OnTime records the working day as it happens: every document, email and web page, connected to the right client matter. It prepares draft time entries for the lawyer’s review and, once they are approved, sends them to billing software such as Clio.
  • OnTrac saves a numbered, labeled copy of every Word and Excel draft each time it is saved, so any version can be found again in seconds. It keeps each matter’s folders in order and tracks every discovery request, response and disclosure. Documents never leave the lawyer’s computer.
  • OnFile checks a filing against the court’s rules, flags anything that should be redacted, and repairs PDFs the court would reject. It then fills in the screens of AZTurboCourt, eFileAZ or federal CM/ECF, attaches the exhibits, and stops for the lawyer to click submit.

“Lawyers lose hours every week to timers, lost drafts and retyping captions into court portals. None of that is the practice of law. We built OnSide so lawyers can work smarter, not harder, and spend their day on the work only a lawyer can do.”

— Nino Abate, founder, OnSide Software, Inc.

The OnTrial Notebook is the ultimate digital trial notebook: one fillable PDF for civil litigators, with scripts for foundations, objections and impeachment; witness, exhibit and deposition pages; proof charts; and jury, trial log and closing pages. Lawyers build it on their computer and take it to court on an iPad or a laptop. It comes in Standard and Professional editions; the Professional edition fills in the rules of Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Nevada, Texas, Utah or Washington, and includes the OnTrial Hearing Notebook.

OnSide designs its products around the working habits and confidentiality duties of the legal profession. Its products do not send what a lawyer types to the company, and its website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. OnTime, OnTrac and OnFile are patent pending.

Availability

OnTime, OnTrac and OnFile are expected to be available for Mac and Windows on December 1, 2026. The OnTrial Notebook is expected to be available the same day for Mac, Windows and iPad. Pricing will be announced before launch at onsidesoftware.com.
