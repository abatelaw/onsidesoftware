How it works

Work the way you already work.

There is no timer to start or stop, and OnTime never interrupts you. Questions wait for your review at the end of the day.

  1. You work

    OnTime runs quietly in the background and notes the document, program or web page in front of you.

  2. OnTime finds the matter

    It works out which client matter the work belongs to. When it cannot tell, it does not guess: it asks you.

  3. You review your day

    You get draft time entries, each with its matter, its time and a one-sentence description. Approve, correct or reject each one.

  4. Approved entries go to Clio

    Each approved entry is entered in Clio once. Nothing reaches a client bill without your approval.

Everything in OnTime

Every feature, at a glance.

Here is everything OnTime does for you at launch, on a Mac or a Windows computer.

Capture

In the background, all day

  • No timers to start or stop
  • Runs quietly and never interrupts you
  • Notes the document, program or web page in front of you
  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDFs and your web browser
  • Recognizes court dockets and Clio matter pages
  • Counts no time while your screen is locked
  • Accounts for every gap in your day

The right matter

Every stretch of work, on the right client

  • Matches each stretch of work to a client matter
  • Setup links your matter folders to your Clio matters; you confirm only the unclear ones
  • Handles clients with several matters
  • Never guesses: when unsure, it asks, with the likely matters first
  • One question covers related work
  • Remembers an answer only when you tell it to
  • Never puts new time on a closed matter

Your daily review

Draft entries, ready to approve

  • Matter, time and a one-sentence description for each entry
  • Measured time beside billable time
  • Your firm’s rounding rule, applied entry by entry
  • Split, merge, discard, or add time by hand
  • Problems listed first, each with its reason
  • Edit any description before you approve
  • Nothing is ever approved for you

Time away from your desk

Court, depositions and calls

  • Connects your Outlook, Google or Clio calendar
  • Offers hearings, depositions, calls and meetings as entries
  • Shows time on no calendar as non-billable
  • Non-billable matters for firm administration
  • A daily record that accounts for every minute
  • The OnTime phone app, coming after launch

Clio

Your billing system stays the same

  • Imports your matters, open and closed
  • Imports your contacts’ email addresses
  • Enters each approved entry once, and checks it
  • Never enters the same entry twice
  • Changes nothing else in Clio
  • Unapproved entries wait for your next review

Privacy and control

Your activity is yours

  • Your activity stays on your computer
  • Only you can see it
  • No AI, screenshots, keystrokes or document contents
  • Never reads the text of your emails
  • Ignores any program or website you choose
  • Pause with one click

Confidentiality

Your activity stays on your computer.

OnTime was designed around client confidentiality. What it records stays on your computer, and only you can see it: there is no view of your activity for your firm or a supervisor. The only thing that leaves is the time entries you approve, on their way to Clio.1

No AI. OnTime follows fixed rules, so the same day gives the same entries, and it invents nothing.

Tell OnTime to ignore programs or websites, such as banking, medical portals or personal email, or pause it with one click.

1 If you turn on an optional Claude connector when one ships, what Claude reads goes to Anthropic under your own Claude account.

What OnTime notes

  • The program in front of you
  • The window title
  • The open document’s folder and file name
  • In a web browser, the page address
  • Whether you are idle or your screen is locked
  • Appointments on the calendars you connect

What it never records

  • The contents of your documents
  • Keystrokes
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Your camera or microphone
  • The text of your emails
  • Where you are

The end-of-day review

Review your day, then send it.

Each entry shows when the work started and ended, the matter, the time OnTime measured, the time to be billed after your rounding rule, and a one-sentence description you can edit.

Nothing is approved for you. Approving one day never approves the next, and an entry you skip comes back at your next review.

  • Mark a matter non-billable, such as your firm’s own administration, and its time is recorded so your day adds up, but it never reaches an invoice.
  • OnTime never puts new time on a closed matter on its own.
  • To correct an entry after it has gone to Clio, change it in Clio.

Coming after launch

Away from your desk? Add it from your phone.

A call from the car, a meeting at the courthouse: open the OnTime companion app on your iPhone, tap Add Call or Add Meeting, pick the client and matter, choose the length, and you’re done. The entry goes to your computer and waits in your daily review with everything else.

  • Two buttons, nothing else to learn: Add Call and Add Meeting.
  • Find the matter by client, matter number or name, with your recent matters first.
  • Quick lengths from 0.1 to 1.0 hour, or exact minutes. Add a note if you like.
  • No timer and no reminders: you add an entry when you choose to.
  • Works offline and sends when it is connected again.
  • Pairs with your computer once, by pointing your iPhone’s camera at a code on the screen. Entries are encrypted end to end on their way.
  • iPhone first; Android to follow.
