How OnTime works

  1. It tracks your time automaticallyOnTime recognizes when you’re working in Word, Excel, your email, your web browser or Claude, and keeps the time on each.
  2. It connects your work activity to the right client matterWhen it can’t tell which matter, it asks instead of guessing.
  3. It drafts accurate time entries for your review and approvalEach with its matter, its time and a short description.
  4. It sends approved entries to your billing softwareDirectly into Clio, MyCase or PracticePanther, or as an export file for another billing program.

No screenshots No keystrokes No document contents

Designed around client confidentiality

OnTime notes what you are working in, never what it says. Its record stays on your computer, and only you can see it.
