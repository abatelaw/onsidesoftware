No Time Left Behind.

OnTime makes timekeeping effortless! It records your billable activity as you work: the documents, apps and web pages you work in, connected to the right client matter. It prepares draft time entries for your review and, once you approve them, sends them to Clio. It’s not only easy; it can also increase your profits by capturing billable time you would otherwise miss.

  • No more tedious manual timers
  • Each item connected to the right client matter
  • Nothing is billed until you approve it
