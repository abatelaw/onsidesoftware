How OnTrial works

  1. You build your case copyFill it in on your computer. Type the case name once and it fills every page.
  2. You find any page fastTwelve tabs, like a paper trial binder, from Prep to Closing.
  3. You take it to courtOpen it on an iPad or a laptop, in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader or PDF Viewer by Nutrient.
  4. You use it on your feetTap each script step as you say it, and log the trial as it happens.
