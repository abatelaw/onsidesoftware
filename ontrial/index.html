Inside the OnTrial Notebook

A tab for every stage of trial.

Twelve tabs down the right edge, like a paper trial binder: Home, Guide, Prep, Proof, Witnesses, Depositions, Exhibits, Exam, Jury, Log, Closing and Notes. These are real pages from the notebook.

Built for the courtroom

Fast to fill in, quick to find.

Type it once

Type the case name once and it fills every page. A witness typed on the Roster fills that witness’s Profile, Direct and Cross pages.

Three bookmarks, one tap

Bookmark the pages you keep going back to, and jump to any of them from any page. Each tab from Prep to Closing opens on an index of big buttons.

Dictate instead of typing

Speak into any field on the iPad, a Mac or a Windows computer, using the dictation already built into your device.

Your state’s rules beside the federal rules

Wherever a page recites a rule, it gives the federal cite and your state’s rule beside it: filled in, in the Professional edition; a blank for you to fill, in Standard.

Runs in apps you already have

The free Adobe Acrobat Reader on a Mac or Windows computer, and PDF Viewer by Nutrient on the iPad. Build it on your computer; take it to court on an iPad or a laptop.

A paper backup when you need it

Print the whole notebook the night before trial. In the Professional edition, a Print button on each list page prints just that list.

What you type stays on your device.1 The User Manual walks through every page.

The OnTrial Hearing Notebook

Argue from one page.

For motions and evidentiary hearings. The Speaking Outline is a chalkboard: your opening word for word, three to seven points in a few keywords each, and your close. Glance down, say it, look up.

  • Tap a point to open its Preparation Outline: the point in full, with up to eight facts and citations.
  • Every fact and citation gets its own row on the Sources and Legal Authorities pages, with its pin cite and a link.
  • A Rebuttal page of the arguments you expect, ticked as the other side makes them.
  • The Record, and copies of your key cases and papers inside the notebook, so they open without a connection.

Included with the Professional edition, and sold on its own.
