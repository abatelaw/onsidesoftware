Why lawyers choose OnFile

File faster, and pay only when you file.

E-filing should not mean retyping a caption into a portal, fixing a PDF the court bounced, or wondering whether a filing went through. OnFile does the clerical work, and you stay in charge of every filing.

1

No subscription fee

Pay $6 only when you file, itemized by case so you can bill it to your client. No charge if the clerk rejects a filing. Any number of lawyers in your firm may use it.

2

No more retyping

OnFile reads the caption and fills in every screen of AZTurboCourt, eFileAZ or CM/ECF: the court, the case, the filer, the document type, the attachments and the service list.

3

Problems caught before the clerk sees them

Type size, margins, page limits, searchable text, file size and more, each check shown with its rule and its source.

Scanned pages made searchable, oversized files reduced, security settings removed, and PDFs repaired that CM/ECF would refuse: on copies, never your originals.

How a filing goes

Drop the files. Review one screen. You submit.

OnFile does the typing and the clicking in the court’s own e-filing system, under your own login. You make every decision that matters.

  1. Drop the files

    The pleading as a PDF, any exhibits, and a proposed order in Word. OnFile reads the caption and puts them in filing order.

  2. Review one screen

    The case, the documents and every check, problems first. A line above Submit says exactly where the filing goes and whether it will be filed.

  3. OnFile fills the screens

    It signs in with your saved login and goes through the portal screen by screen: court, case, filer, document type, attachments and service list. You can watch.

  4. You pay and submit

    OnFile stops at the payment page. You check the fee and submit. OnFile never pays a fee for you.

Everything in OnFile

Every feature, at a glance.

Here is everything OnFile does for you at launch, on a Mac or a Windows computer.

Before you file

One review screen

  • Drop the pleading, exhibits and proposed order in one go
  • Reads the court and case from the caption
  • Puts the documents in filing order
  • Add more exhibits later by drag and drop
  • Scanned pages made searchable
  • The court’s technical rules checked, each with its rule and source
  • Problems first, marked Block, Warn or Fix
  • PDFs repaired on copies; your own files never changed
  • Personal identifiers and confidential pages flagged
  • Your side and the service list remembered for the case

Filing

Every screen filled for you

  • AZTurboCourt and eFileAZ for Arizona’s state courts
  • CM/ECF for the District of Arizona’s District and Bankruptcy Courts
  • Signs in with your own saved login
  • Fills in the court, case, filer, document type, attachments and service list
  • Starts new civil cases through AZTurboCourt
  • Shows exactly where a filing goes before you submit
  • Practice runs that file nothing
  • Stops and tells you what to do when it meets something it should not decide
  • You check the fee and pay it; OnFile never pays
  • In CM/ECF, proposes the event, the party and the related docket entry
  • Lists anyone CM/ECF does not serve, for service by mail

After filing

Nothing slips through

  • A Filings tab with every filing and the court’s filing number
  • Status checks with the court until a filing is accepted or rejected
  • A repeating alarm, on screen and by email, if a filing fails or is rejected
  • The clerk’s reasons for a rejection, where the portal gives them
  • Each case saved with its parties, judge and service list
  • Cases, filings and alarms shared with your firm, encrypted so OnSide cannot read them
  • Court passwords kept in your computer’s own password store
  • A receipt for every filing, by case, to bill your client
  • No charge for a rejected filing

Fixed rules, your login

No AI. The same files always give the same result.

OnFile follows fixed rules. It never reviews the content or legal sufficiency of a document, and when it cannot tell something, it asks you or hands you the portal window with a note saying what to do.

Your filing goes only to the court’s e-filing system, under your own login. Your court passwords stay in your computer’s own password store, the Mac Keychain or Windows Credential Manager, never on OnSide’s servers. Each person files under their own login, never a shared one.

You always

  • Choose what is filed
  • See where it goes before you submit
  • Check the fee and pay it
  • Type your own sign-in codes

OnFile never

  • Pays a fee for you
  • Changes your original files
  • Guesses
  • Keeps your court password on OnSide’s servers
  • Uses AI

Courts at launch

Arizona’s state and federal courts.

At launch, OnFile files in Arizona’s state courts through AZTurboCourt and eFileAZ, and in the U.S. District Court and Bankruptcy Court for the District of Arizona through CM/ECF.

You need your own login for each portal you file through, and a PACER account for CM/ECF. Court and filing-provider fees are paid on the portal’s own payment page, as they are today.

  • Every filing is listed on the Filings tab, with the court’s filing number and who clicked Submit.
  • Each case is saved with its first filing: court, number, parties, judge, your side and its service list.
  • A receipt for every filing, with the case number and matter, so you can bill OnFile’s fee to your client as a case cost.
