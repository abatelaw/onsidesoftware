E-filing made easy.

OnFile reads your document, checks it against the court’s filing rules, flags anything that should be redacted, and repairs PDFs the court would reject. Then it fills in every screen of AZTurboCourt, eFileAZ or CM/ECF for Arizona’s federal courts, attaches your exhibits, and stops for you to review and submit.

  • No more retyping captions into the portal
  • No more filings bounced for a bad PDF
  • You always press submit yourself
