How OnFile works

  1. You drop in the filingThe pleading, its exhibits and a proposed order. OnFile reads the caption and puts them in filing order.
  2. It checks the court’s rulesType size, margins, page limits, searchable text and more, with problems listed first.
  3. It fills in the court’s screensCourt, case, filer, document type, attachments and service list, under your own login.
  4. You review, pay and submitOnFile stops at the payment page. It never pays a fee for you.

Better design equals better results

OnFile was designed for Arizona lawyers who file their own papers. It files in Arizona’s state courts, which none of the three services below listed as available in November 2026. It checks each filing by fixed rules that need no AI, and flags what should be redacted before you submit.
