How the conversations work

  1. Choose where you are242 lessons in 24 everyday scenes: the airport, the market, the pharmacy, renting an apartment, telling your own stories.
  2. Say your partSpeak or type. Your partner stays in role, in casual tú or polite usted, at the level you pick.
  3. Get it right the Mexican wayWhen you slip, you see what a Mexican would say and why, and your own mistakes come back as quick speaking drills.
