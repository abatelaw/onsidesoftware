MXNOW: Mexican Spanish

iPhone and Android · Coming to the App Store and Google Play

Speak Mexican Spanish out loud, in the places you will need it. Pick a scene, from the taquería to renting an apartment, and say your part. Your AI conversation partner answers in role, and when you slip, it shows you what a Mexican would say and why.

  • 242 conversation lessons in 24 everyday scenes, in casual tú or polite usted
  • For every level: you pick beginner, intermediate or advanced
  • Oído mexicano: real recordings of three native speakers from Mexico City, Culiacán and Ciudad Juárez
  • 1,000 everyday words, 41 modismos and short grammar rules with Mexican examples
  • Going to Mexico? The 30-Day Travel Pass gives full access for 30 days with one payment and nothing to cancel, plus Plan de viaje, a day-by-day plan built around 15 minutes a day
  • No account to create; your progress stays on your phone
