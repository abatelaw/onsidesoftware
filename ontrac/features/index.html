DiscoverTracker

Discovery and disclosure

  • Every request, response, disclosure, production and deposition in one place
  • RFPs, interrogatories and RFAs for each side, set by set
  • Due dates and timeliness worked out for you
  • Each side’s requests counted against Arizona’s Rule 26.2 limits
  • Colors for on time, due soon, late, open or never disclosed
  • A To Check list of the rows to look at
  • A chronology of every event, with its source and pin cite
  • Deadlines, disputes, meet-and-confer and rulings
  • Coverage: what you need, how you asked, what you got
  • Deposition errata deadlines worked out
  • Interrogatory answers without a signed verification flagged
  • Notes, document links and highlights on any row
  • The full text of each request and response, one click away
  • Objection grounds ticked from a list
  • Pick from menus instead of typing; IDs made for you
  • Undo and redo
  • Export to Excel, with an Outstanding tab
  • One owner per case; colleagues see it read-only

DocTracker

Versions of your Word and Excel drafts

  • One keystroke saves a numbered version
  • Labels: dv for a draft, fv for a final, exe for ready to sign
  • Your working file always shows its current label
  • Every earlier version kept in a vault folder inside the client’s folder
  • Restore any earlier version
  • A draft line on each page in Word, removed when final
  • Your own footer never changed
  • Returned drafts from co-counsel or opposing counsel kept as they arrived
  • Ordinary saves backed up between versions
  • Warns you if it ever stops keeping versions
  • A color tag in Finder for each label on a Mac
  • Removing OnTrac leaves every version in place

FileTracker

Folders, PDFs and matter files

  • Sticky Folder: Save As opens in the folder you are working in
  • Or keep Save As in one folder while you work on one brief
  • Save as PDF Here: one keystroke, same name and label
  • Choose where PDFs go: beside the document, in Pleadings, or ask each time
  • Organize a matter: litigation, personal injury, or business and real estate layout
  • A plan of every move, with its reason, before anything moves
  • Files it cannot place are left for you to place
  • A history of every job, with a timed Undo
  • A templates folder in every matter
  • Each matter’s caption pleading paper made from your firm’s master
  • Close a matter, with an optional hand-over copy for the client or new counsel
  • Files move only inside their own matter folder
