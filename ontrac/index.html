DiscoverTracker

Know where discovery stands, every day.

DiscoverTracker keeps a case’s disclosure and discovery in one place. It works out the due dates, whether each response was on time, and how each side’s requests count against the limits. You add what it cannot know, such as a deadline a stipulation moved.

  • Colors show what was done on time, what is due soon, and what is late, open or never disclosed. A To Check list shows what needs you.
  • You almost never type: pick a code from a menu, a date from a calendar, a request from a list. OnTrac numbers every row.
  • Chronology, deadlines, disputes, meet-and-confer, rulings and depositions, with the errata deadline worked out.
  • Notes on any row, links to documents in the matter’s folder, and the full text of each request and response.
  • An answer to interrogatories stays flagged until its verification is signed.
  • Export the case to Excel, with a tab listing every request still outstanding.
  • One lawyer owns each case; colleagues see it read-only.

DocTracker

The file in front of you is always the latest draft.

Work in Word or Excel as always. When a draft is worth keeping, press a keyboard shortcut. OnTrac saves the document, keeps the previous version under its label, and gives your working file its new label, such as Demand Letter (dv04).docx.

  • Three labels: dv for a draft, fv for a final, exe for a final ready to sign.
  • Every earlier version is kept in the matter’s own vault folder, one folder away. Restore any of them.
  • A draft in Word carries a small draft line at the foot of each page. It comes off when you mark the document final; your own footer is never changed.
  • A draft that comes back from co-counsel or a client is kept as it arrived, and the next version is marked as including their edits.
  • Your ordinary saves are backed up in between, and removing OnTrac leaves every version where it is.

FileTracker

New files go where they belong.

FileTracker works in the background so a document saves back to the folder it came from, and, when you ask, it organizes your client matters into a standard set of folders.

  • Sticky Folder. Save As and Save as PDF open in the folder of the document you are working on, not the last folder you happened to use.
  • Save as PDF Here. One keystroke puts a PDF beside the document, with the same name and label. No dialog.
  • Organize a matter. Choose a layout (litigation, personal injury, or business and real estate). OnTrac shows you a plan of what would move and why, and moves nothing until you apply it. Undo is in the History.
  • Files move only inside their own matter folder, and organizing deletes nothing.
  • Close a matter. Move it to your closed folder, and make a clean hand-over copy for the client or new counsel.
