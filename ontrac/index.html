Three Problems. One Solution.

OnTrac tracks your discovery, saves a numbered, labeled version of a Word or Excel draft with one keystroke, and keeps your matter folders in order. It runs quietly in the background, and your documents never leave your computer.1

  • Discovery tracking: every request, response and disclosure, with its due date
  • Document versioning: one keystroke saves a numbered, labeled version you can go back to
  • File organizing: new files put where they belong

1 If you turn on the optional Claude connector when it ships, what Claude reads goes to Anthropic under your own Claude account.
