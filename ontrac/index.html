How OnTrac works

  1. It tracks every discovery request and deadlineDiscoverTracker works out the due dates and shows what is due soon, late or never answered.
  2. It saves a numbered version with one keystrokeDocTracker keeps every earlier version of your Word or Excel draft, and you can restore any of them.
  3. It keeps your matter folders in orderFileTracker opens Save As in the folder you are working in and puts each PDF beside its document.

No AI No uploads No file deleted without your OK

Your documents stay yours

OnTrac works in your own folders and never sends your documents anywhere. Before it organizes a matter it shows you the plan, and you can undo it.
