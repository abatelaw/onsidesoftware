Available December 1, 2026 · Mac, Windows and iPad

A trial notebook for civil litigators, in one fillable PDF. Scripts for foundations, objections and impeachment; witness, exhibit and deposition pages; proof charts; jury, trial log and closing pages; a tab for every stage of trial. Build it on your computer and take it to court on an iPad or a laptop. What you type stays on your device.

Standard

  • The Federal Rules of Evidence and Civil Procedure, with a blank for your state’s rule beside each
  • 10 witnesses, 6 claims, 85 and 51 exhibits, 5 trial days
  • Bookmarks, sticky notes, and a case name that fills every page
  • Pages locked, so the notebook stays in order
Available December 1, 2026

Professional

  • Everything in Standard, at double the size: 20 witnesses, 10 claims, 170 and 102 exhibits, 10 trial days
  • Your state’s rules filled in, with notes where your state differs: Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Nevada, Texas, Utah or Washington; more states can be added
  • Add your own pages; print any list
  • OnTrial Export: exhibit lists, witness list, deposition designations and a proof gap report in Word and Excel
  • Includes the OnTrial Hearing Notebook
Available December 1, 2026

Runs in Adobe Acrobat Reader on Mac or Windows and in PDF Viewer by Nutrient on the iPad, both free. One license per lawyer. All sales are final. See the Terms of Service.
